# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are recruiters evaluating Benjamin Sánchez for junior web and mobile developer opportunities. Potential collaborators may also use the portfolio to understand his work and direction.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Benjamin Sánchez that presents his development background, selected work, skills, and current direction toward Blockchain and Web3. Success means giving recruiters a credible, clear way to assess his fit and contact him.

## Positioning

Benjamin is a junior developer with DAW and DAM training who demonstrates real web and mobile project work while being transparent about actively developing toward Blockchain and Web3 rather than claiming expertise in it.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse a bilingual (Spanish and English) portfolio, review projects and their media, download a CV, follow social links, and can send Benjamin a message through the contact form.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A TanStack Start / React web application with i18next localization.
- Sections cover Benjamin's profile, skills, projects, Blockchain/Web3 learning direction, and contact.
- The contact form is backed by Resend and requires the configured server-only environment variables for delivery.
- Project details, wording, and claims must remain truthful; Blockchain and Web3 are an active learning direction, not an asserted professional specialization.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Benjamin Sánchez portfolio.
- Content is available in Spanish and English.
- Preserve the existing GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, CV, project media, and InternHub Excellence Award evidence.

## Product Principles

- Make Benjamin's real work and demonstrated learning easy to verify.
- Speak plainly and accurately about junior-level experience and current goals.
- Keep the recruiter journey concise: understand the profile, inspect work, access the CV, and get in touch.
- Preserve bilingual access to the same product truth.
